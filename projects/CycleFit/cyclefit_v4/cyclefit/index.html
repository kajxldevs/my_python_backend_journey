The science

Your hormones change everything.

Days 1–5

Menstrual

Your body is shedding the uterine lining. Energy often dips, and rest becomes essential. This is not weakness — it is biology asking you to slow down and recover.

Days 6–13

Follicular

Estrogen rises and energy returns. Your body is primed to build strength and try new movements. Workouts that felt impossible last week may feel natural now.

Days 14–16

Ovulatory

Estrogen peaks alongside a surge in testosterone. Many women feel their strongest here — faster, more powerful, ready for intensity. This is your window for peak performance.

Days 17–28

Luteal

Progesterone rises, which can increase fatigue and water retention. Your body shifts toward maintenance. Steady, moderate work serves you better than pushing to your limit.

What CycleFit does

Built around your biology.

Workout plans change every week based on your cycle, so you train with your hormones instead of fighting them.
